# CRP-Time algorithms: labels

Status 26 Nov 2026, after Thom's decisions on the open points of the first labelling round (`64bea69`, `6d3841b`). One row per CRP-Time algorithm: the ones offered in the Workbench for CRP-Time, the unregistered multi-bay versions, and the two old multi-bay classes that are no longer registered. Labels shown in the Workbench are class attributes (`core/algorithm_meta.py`): `relocation_rule`, `geometry` (scope), `time_category` (category), `validation_status` with `validation_evidence`, and `fidelity` (adapted flag). An undeclared label is not shown.

**Labels describe the implementation.** Where the paper differs, the evidence gives the paper's version and says that the implementation deviates.

## Definitions (fixed, 26 Nov 2026)

- **Relocation rule.** Restricted: only containers above the current target are relocated. Unrestricted: otherwise. Labels give the implementation's rule.
- **Category.** 1: the selection rules or the search use crane travel or time, exact or as a distance proxy (`time-in-selection`). 2: selection uses only relocation, priority or move-count information, and crane time is evaluated afterwards (`time-afterwards`). A tie-break is not a selection criterion.
- **Validation status.**
  - Validated: passed its pre-registered criterion.
  - Partially validated: implemented per paper, criterion not met.
  - Checked post hoc: compared against the paper's reported results with a documented numeric comparison, but no criterion fixed beforehand.
  - Not validated: otherwise.

  Every status cites the document and section where the criterion or comparison and its result are recorded. Without such a citation the status is not validated. The Workbench shows the definition and the evidence as the tooltip of the status chip.
- **Adapted.** A deliberate change to the algorithm's logic beyond replacing its objective with the unified f2/f2vert (`fidelity = "adapted"`). Converting the paper's own objective, including the bounds that are derived from it, is not by itself an adaptation.

Document paths are relative to `docs/validation/`. `data/<folder>/README.md` is in the nested validation data repository.

## Decisions (Thom, 26 Nov 2026)

These replace the open points S1, R1, R2/L1 and A1 of the first round (`6d3841b`).

- **S1, status of Azari, Jovanović, Difference1 and Difference2.** The status "checked post hoc" was added. A log with a documented numeric comparison against the paper's reported results gets "checked post hoc", otherwise "not validated".
  - Jovanović: checked post hoc (Tables 1 and 5, §4).
  - Azari: not validated. The log reports that the paper's numerical results could not be checked (Summary; §4, Check 3). The worked examples (Fig. 1, Fig. 4) are reproduced, but they are worked examples, not reported results.
  - Difference1 and Difference2: not validated, for the same reason (O11).
- **R1, Lee–Lee.** Labels describe the implementation, so Lee–Lee is restricted. The evidence names the paper's rule (unrestricted, Example 1, p. 1145) and says that the implementation deviates.
- **R2 and L1, the three CRP_R heuristics.** Restricted, category 2 and not validated, declared in their CRP_R classes. The labels are true under CRP-R as well. The question to Wei about them is withdrawn.
- **A1, adapted.** Adapted means a deliberate change to the algorithm's logic beyond replacing its objective with f2/f2vert. Applied to every row; only Jovanović changes (faithful → adapted, LB_f). López-Plata and Firmino stay not adapted. Both Forster–Bortfeldt restricted versions and Lee–Lee stay adapted.
- **C1, Difference1.** Stays category 2. A tie-break is not a selection criterion; the evidence records the tie-break to the lowest stack index (O3).
